# ADR-TRT2-001 (DRAFT): Promotion of triadic-reality-theory-v2 to canonical

**Status: draft. Not approved. Approval is JD's alone.**

## Context

V1 remains the authoritative historical baseline. V2 was stood up as a candidate on 2026-11-22 with typed claim discipline, separated physical admissibility, and explicit theorem obligations TO-1 through TO-6.

## Gates

- G1 Foundational integrity: foundations internally consistent, categories separated, integrity findings from v1 dispositioned or visibly open.
- G2 TRT-M0 conditional TO-1 proof, with full dependency and axiom audit.
- G3 Countermodels and negative tests: the countermodel suite runs and its failures are classified.
- G4 Evidence and claim traceability: every registry claim traces to an artifact and a source.
- G5 EBCC compliance, independent review, and JD approval of this ADR.

TO-2 through TO-6 need not be solved for promotion, but must remain visibly open in the registry at promotion.

## Decision (to be recorded on approval)

On JD's approval, v2 becomes canonical, v1 is retained as the historical baseline (not deleted, not overwritten), and CITATION.cff in both repositories is updated to point citation at the canonical line.
