# Ontology and types

Lead: WP-TRT2-001.

## Types

- `Sigma-infinity`: syntactic representables (terms, configurations, descriptions).
- `Omega_L3`: semantic models satisfying L3, related to syntax by a satisfaction relation.
- Interpretation map: quantum physical states map to semantic models by an explicit interpretation map. A physical state is not an untyped literal subset of semantic models.
- `D`: the actualizable domain, defined by L3-admissibility plus the separately typed physical admissibility constraint.
- `X / chi`: actualized reality, the range of the actualization operator A0 over admissible inputs.

## Rules

Every new entity introduced in v2 work declares its type and the maps it participates in. Untyped subset or identity claims between physical and semantic domains are a review failure.
