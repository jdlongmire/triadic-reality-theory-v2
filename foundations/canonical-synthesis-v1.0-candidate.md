## The triad

Determinate actuality requires three irreducible co-primitives:

1. **L3**: the laws of logic as ontological constraint (Identity, Non-Contradiction, Excluded Middle). L3 supplies no content and no transition.
2. **I-infinity**: unbounded informational representability. It supplies differentiation. A contradiction may be representable without being actualizable.
3. **A**: fundamental action, primitive state transition, without presupposing physical time, motion, force, energy, or actuality. Ordered A-relations may ground derivative temporal succession.

representable, then L3-admissible, then actualizable, then actual. These are typed stages, not assumed equivalences. Whether L3-coherence alone suffices for actualizability is an open obligation, not an axiom of v2.
