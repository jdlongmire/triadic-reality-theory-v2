# Sequential actualization

Actualization is modelled as a partial, history-preserving operator: partial, because not every admissible input actualizes; history-preserving, because the actualized record retains the sequence of prior actualizations on which consistency conditions (TO-1) are stated. Admissibility rules for the operator are independent of the operator itself and are stated separately.

Operational quantum instruments and POVM updates describe measurement statistics and state update rules. They are not identified with ontological actualization. Any claimed correspondence is a theorem obligation, not a definition.
