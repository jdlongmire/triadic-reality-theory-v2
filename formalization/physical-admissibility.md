# Physical admissibility

Physical admissibility is a separately typed constraint on the actualizable domain, additional to L3-admissibility. It is where dynamical and relativistic requirements live:

- No-signaling: a requirement in its own right.
- Lorentz covariance: a requirement in its own right.
- Microcausality: a requirement in its own right.

None of the three is discharged by asserting another. TO-3 tracks all three; TO-6 tracks admissibility and dynamical closure as a whole.

Recorded corrections, binding on derivations: partial trace does not generally erase all local coherence; decoherence does not by itself solve outcome selection.
